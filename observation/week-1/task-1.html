About ANITS

sri chiatanya techno school was established in the academic year 1986 under the sri chaitanya educational Institutions. Sri Chaitanya Educational Institutions is a group of education institutions chain of schools, junior colleges, hostels, Sri Chaitanya launched the Infinity Learn app in 2022, with educational content for classes 6–12 with IIT-JEE, NEET and CBSE board exam coaching.[7] In 2022, it acquired the edtech platform Don't Memorise[8] and a 75% stake in WizKlub.[9] In December 2024, Infinity Learn partnered with Google Cloud India to utilize artificial intelligence (AI) technologies.coaching institutions for IIT-JEE, NEET, EAMCET and UPSC-CS preparation and degree colleges across India. The academic director of Sri Chaitanya is Sushma Bopanna.
